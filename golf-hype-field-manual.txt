2. DEVICE SETUP (Set It & Forget It)
==================================================

We are shooting MOST shots at 60fps. Some shots require SLOW-MO mode (explained below).

--- STANDARD 60FPS SETUP ---

IPHONE (iOS 17+):
1. Settings > Camera > Record Video > Select 4K at 60 fps
2. Camera Settings > Preserve Settings > Turn ON Exposure Adjustment

ANDROID (Samsung):
1. Camera App > Video Mode > Resolution > Select UHD (4K) 60
2. Turn ON "Super Steady" (Hand icon) if walking

GOPRO:
1. Res/FPS: 4K / 60
2. Lens: Linear + Horizon Leveling


--- WHEN TO USE SLOW-MO MODE ---

Some shots below are marked "TECH: SLOW-MO" - for ONLY those shots, switch modes:

IPHONE:
- Swipe to "SLO-MO" mode (shoots at 240fps automatically)
- After the shot, swipe BACK to regular Video mode

ANDROID:
- Swipe to "SLOW MOTION" mode (shoots at 120-240fps depending on phone)
- After the shot, swipe BACK to Video mode

GOPRO:
- Change frame rate to 120fps or 240fps
- After the shot, change BACK to 60fps


--- HOW TO LOCK FOCUS/EXPOSURE ---

For shots that say "Focus lock" or need steady exposure:

IPHONE:
- Tap and hold on the subject until "AE/AF LOCK" appears at the top
- A yellow box will stay on screen

ANDROID:
- Tap the subject, then tap the lock icon that appears
- Icon will turn yellow/locked

GOPRO:
- Tap screen on the subject (locks automatically)

==================================================
1. The "Default" is Horizontal

2. The "Double Tap" Method (For Vertical)

A. HERO & ACTION (The "Sport")
--------------------------------------------------
[ ] The "Worm Burner" (Low Angle Drive)
    SETUP: Lay phone on grass 3 feet behind the tee. Prop it against a tee or rock so it points UP at the golfer.
    ACTION: Golfer hits a full driver swing over the camera.
    TIMING: Start recording BEFORE they step up to the ball. Hold for 5 seconds AFTER the swing.
    - You can get this shot with as many golfers as you want for a possible compilation of drives.
    TECH: 4K/60 | VERTICAL ALSO: YES

[ ] The Swing Follow
    SETUP: Stand 6ft behind the golfer. Hold phone horizontal at chest height.
    ACTION: Golfer hits the ball.
    TIMING: Start 3 seconds before swing. Twist your body to follow the ball into the sky. Hold on the empty sky for 3 seconds.
    - Everyone who did the previous shot should also do this shot.
    TECH: 4K/60 | VERTICAL ALSO: NO

[ ] Tee Smash (POV)
    SETUP: Hold camera 1 foot behind the ball (safely!). Get low. Focus lock on the logo of the golf ball.
    ACTION: Golfer hits the ball (it will disappear instantly).
    TIMING: Hit record. Wait 3 seconds. Golfer hits. Wait 3 seconds. Cut.
    - Same golfers as previous shot. Different version for dynamic editing.
    TECH: SLOW-MO | VERTICAL ALSO: YES

[ ] Ball Flight Tracking
    SETUP: Stand beside the golfer. Point camera at the sky where the ball will go.
    ACTION: Track the ball's arc from swing to landing. Follow it smoothly with the camera.
    TIMING: Start recording before swing. Follow the ball through the entire flight. Hold 2 seconds after landing.
    - This is tricky but looks amazing when done right. Try it a few times.
    TECH: 4K/60 | VERTICAL ALSO: NO

[ ] Sand Trap Blast
    SETUP: Crouch low OUTSIDE the bunker (keep phone safe from sand). Do NOT zoom.
    ACTION: Golfer hits ball out of sand. Ideally, a wall of sand flies up.
    TIMING: Start 3 seconds before swing. Hold until the sand settles on the ground.
    - Sand action would be nice for transitions. If you can't get close enough safely, skip this shot.
    TECH: SLOW-MO | VERTICAL ALSO: NO

[ ] The Divot
    SETUP: Lay phone on the ground 2 feet in front of where the ball sits. Get LOW.
    ACTION: Golfer takes swing and club rips out a chunk of grass toward camera.
    TIMING: Start 3 seconds before swing. Hold 3 seconds after grass flies.
    - Dramatic shot. Make sure phone is safe from club contact.
    TECH: SLOW-MO | VERTICAL ALSO: NO

[ ] The Orbit
    SETUP: Golfer stands perfectly still holding their driver like a sword/cane.
    ACTION: Shooter walks in a SLOW circle around the golfer (360 degrees). Take 20 seconds for full circle.
    TIMING: Walk heel-to-toe like a ninja. Try to keep the golfer in the center of the frame the whole time.
    - Can do this for as many golfers or 'faces' you want to be shown possibly.
    TECH: 4K/60 | VERTICAL ALSO: YES

[ ] Silhouette (Golden Hour Only)
    SETUP: Find an angle where the Sun is directly BEHIND the golfer.
    ACTION: Golfer takes a practice swing.
    TIMING: Record the dark outline of the golfer against the bright sun. Hold for 10 seconds.
    - Only works during golden hour (sunrise/sunset). Subject will be completely dark.
    TECH: 4K/60 | VERTICAL ALSO: YES

[ ] Club Selection
    SETUP: Get close to golf bag. Focus on the hands.
    ACTION: Golfer reaches into bag and pulls out a specific club.
    TIMING: Start recording before hand enters frame. Hold 2 seconds after club is pulled out.
    - Nice detail shot that tells story.
    TECH: 4K/60 | VERTICAL ALSO: NO

    ACTION: Golfer reads the green or stares down the fairway before big shot.

[ ] Group Cheers

5. FINAL REMINDERS
==================================================
* STABILITY: "T-Rex Arms." Keep elbows glued to your ribs. Move your whole body, not just your hands.

* LIGHTING: Keep the sun to your side or behind you. If the subject is a silhouette, move your feet 90 degrees. (Exception: the intentional Silhouette shot)

* DON'T CUT EARLY: Count to 3 seconds AFTER the action finishes before you stop recording.

* AUDIO: For shots marked with sound effects (Rattle, Riiip, Thud, Crack), get within 3 feet of the action. Cover mic with your hand if windy (leave small gap for sound).

* BACKUP YOUR WORK: After every 5-10 shots, AirDrop or upload key clips to cloud storage. If your phone dies or gets damaged, I need SOMETHING to work with. Better to backup 5 times than lose the entire shoot.

* DON'T PANIC: If you're overwhelmed, just shoot the Panic Button section. I can make magic with those 5 shots alone.
