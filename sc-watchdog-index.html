SC Watchdog

Keeping South Carolina Local Governments Accountable

Mission

Government transparency is not a privilege—it's a right. SC Watchdog conducts independent investigations into local government operations across South Carolina to ensure accountability to the citizens they serve.

Every American citizen has the right to know how their tax dollars are spent, how decisions are made, and whether their elected officials are acting in the public interest. This site documents findings from ongoing investigations into municipal operations, contracts, budgets, and governance.

Methodology

All investigations are conducted using publicly available records, FOIA requests, public meeting attendance, and citizen reports. Findings are documented with primary source materials and presented with journalistic integrity.
